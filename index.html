Our space

A quiet corner of Nyarutarama, built to slow you down.

Walk in and you'll find shelves of loose herbs and adaptogens — baobab, lion's mane, moringa, cacao — a nutritive bar plating fresh bowls each morning, and a team that would rather talk about how you're actually feeling than sell you a diet.

BeYou brings together a nutritive bar, one-on-one nutrition coaching, seasonal retreats, healthy talks and hands-on cooking workshops — all under one roof, on 6 KG 552 Street.
